# About

I'm Altanbaatar Enkhsuld, an embedded control software engineer in the power electronics field. My interests include power conversion, control systems, and robotics.

This portfolio brings together my engineering projects, simulations, and technical writeups.

## What you'll find here

- **Projects and build logs:** design details, implementation, and testing.
- **Mini papers:** technical notes, derivations, experiments, and observations.
- **Simulation models:** system behavior and simulation results.
- **Engineering tools:** small utilities for transfer-function analysis and control-related calculations.

Most of the material is based on things I build, test, or simulate. The writeups keep useful calculations, observations, and results together as a technical reference, with the level of detail depending on the topic.
