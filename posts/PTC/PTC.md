This mini paper demonstrates the fundamental differences between software-based timing and hardware-based timing on an STM32F4 microcontroller.
By comparing HAL_Delay(), hardware timer interrupts, and PWM generation, it shows why hardware timers are essential for deterministic timing and real-time control applications.
