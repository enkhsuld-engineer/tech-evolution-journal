This mini paper explains the closed-loop control of a Buck Converter using a cascaded voltage and current control structure.  
A DC-DC buck converter is regulated using two PI controllers arranged in an outer voltage loop and an inner current loop.  
The study focuses on how cascaded control improves voltage regulation, current tracking, and overall system stability through simulation.
